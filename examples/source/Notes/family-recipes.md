# Family recipes

## Tomato and lentil soup

- 2 tins chopped tomatoes
- 200 g red lentils
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika

Simmer for 25 minutes. Add lemon and black pepper before serving.

## Garden herb dressing

Whisk chopped parsley, basil, lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of salt.
